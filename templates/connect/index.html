Connect your AI

Let an AI assistant plan, draft and schedule your social posts, within the limits you set

{{ brand_name }} runs an MCP server, so any AI tool that supports MCP connectors can work with your workspace. You sign in and choose what the assistant may do on an approval screen. Anything it does is limited by those choices and by your workspace's autonomy setting, which can hold posts for a person to review.
