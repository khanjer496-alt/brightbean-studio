2. How we use data and our legal bases

If the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Contract: to provide the service, publish your posts, show your inbox and analytics, process payments and support you.
  • Legitimate interests: to secure and debug the service, prevent abuse, and improve reliability, balanced against your rights.
  • Legal obligation: tax, accounting and responding to lawful requests.
  • Consent: where requested, for example when you connect a platform account and approve its permissions; you can withdraw it by disconnecting.

3. AI processing

When you use the AI writer or AI reply drafts, the content you submit, and related context such as the message being answered or your workspace's brand voice, is sent to our AI model provider, Anthropic, to generate a response. According to the provider's commercial terms, content submitted through its API is not used to train its models; we do not control the provider's terms, so please review them. If your organization saves its own AI key, requests are made under your own agreement with the provider. AI features are optional and off unless used.

4. Information from connected platforms

We use data from a platform only to provide the features you ask for, and we follow each platform's developer policies. We do not use it to train generalized AI models, to advertise, or sell it to data brokers.

Google and YouTube

{{ brand_name }}'s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This covers data from YouTube and Google Business Profile. We use it only to provide and improve the user-facing features you request, do not transfer it except as needed to provide those features, to comply with law or as part of a merger, do not use it for advertising, and do not allow humans to read it except with your consent, for security, or to comply with law.

The service uses YouTube API Services. By using these features you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke our access to your Google data at any time through the Google security settings page. Data obtained through YouTube API Services is refreshed or deleted within 30 days, and disconnecting a YouTube channel in {{ brand_name }} deletes the data we hold from it and revokes the grant.

Meta (Facebook, Instagram, Threads)

We receive Page, profile, media, comment, message and insight data you authorize and handle it in line with the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies. You can remove our access in your Facebook or Instagram settings under Business Integrations, or by disconnecting the account here. If you remove the app, Meta may notify us and we delete the associated data; see Data deletion.

Other platforms

TikTok, LinkedIn, Pinterest, X, Reddit, Discord, Slack, Telegram, Bluesky, Mastodon and DEV.to are handled the same way: we request only the permissions needed for the features you use, store the tokens encrypted, and revoke or delete them when you disconnect, where the platform offers a way to do so. Your use of those platforms is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Who receives data

We share data only with the service providers needed to run {{ brand_name }}, listed on the Subprocessors page: hosting, CDN and storage, payments, AI processing and email delivery{% if sentry_enabled %}, and error monitoring{% endif %}. We also disclose data to the platforms you publish to, to your teammates according to their roles, and where required by law or to protect rights and safety. If we are involved in a merger or sale, data may transfer subject to this policy.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

We use only what is needed to run the service: a session cookie that keeps you signed in, a CSRF cookie that protects forms, a short-lived timezone cookie set on the sign-up page, and a local browser setting remembering whether the sidebar is collapsed. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. Pages also load some open source scripts from a public CDN (see Licenses), which means that CDN receives your IP address.

7. Retention

  • Account and workspace content is kept while your account is active.
  • When you delete your account, personal data is deleted immediately, and organizations you alone belong to are deleted with their content. Encrypted backups are overwritten on their normal rotation schedule.
  • If an organization deletion is scheduled, it runs after a 14 day grace period.
  • Billing records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Server logs are kept for a limited period for security and debugging.

8. Deleting your data

You can delete your account in Settings, or ask us by email. Full details, including Meta's deletion callback, are on the Data deletion page.
