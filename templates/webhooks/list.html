Get a signed HTTPS request at your own server when something happens in {{ workspace.name }}: a post goes out or fails, an approval is decided, a message arrives. Each request carries a PostDelegate-Signature header, an HMAC-SHA256 of the timestamp and body keyed with the endpoint’s secret, so your server can check it came from us. Endpoints can also be managed through the REST API.
