# Listing text (mcp.so, Glama, Smithery, others)

- **Name:** PostDelegate
- **One line (under 100 characters):** Plan, draft, schedule and review social posts, inbox replies and analytics, with approvals.
- **Description:** PostDelegate is a social media scheduler with a remote MCP server. Agents can read the calendar and connected accounts, draft per-network posts in the saved brand voice, find the best time and next free slot, schedule or send posts for approval, post threads, repurpose a URL, triage inbox comments and DMs, read analytics and reports, edit link-in-bio pages and manage evergreen recycling. 128 tools in 11 capability groups; each tool is annotated read-only, destructive, idempotent and open-world. A workspace autonomy setting decides what an agent may do alone (default: drafts only, a person approves), and the owner narrows an agent's capabilities on an OAuth consent screen.
- **URL:** https://app.postdelegate.com/api/v1/mcp (Streamable HTTP)
- **Auth:** OAuth 2.1 with PKCE and dynamic client registration; bearer tokens also supported
